Add `--rank-bias B` to the frontier runner: every choice of a node (revisits, parents, catalog ideas and the routes made of parents) is weighted by 1 / (1 + B * rank / worst rank) on exploration score, so the best node is at most 1 + B times as likely as the worst and none is excluded. All such draws share one picker, `pick_nodes`, which also takes `uniform=True` for a fully random pick. Not combined with `--temperature`.
