The frontier viewer now floats nodes like a graph view and scales to tens of thousands of nodes: a layered, crossing-aware seed layout and a Barnes-Hut force simulation with grid-based overlap removal (n log n), a canvas renderer with zoom-dependent detail (dots, boxes, cards) for graphs over 250 nodes, node details fetched on demand, and a cheap snapshot probe for live refresh. Zoom and pan are smooth, and the node with the lowest held-out MSE for each parameter count is softly highlighted.
