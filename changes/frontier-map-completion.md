Warm starts now complete or repair the model's nesting maps. When a declared map leaves out parameters or doesn't reproduce the parent, a bounded search (64 candidates, one batched check) tries the declared bindings, the parent's parameter with the same or numbered name (`s1`, `s2` from `s`), and the constants 0 and 1. It keeps the first map that reproduces the parent's predictions. Unverified name matching remains the last resort. On the two earlier runs this finds a correct map for 14 of 33 parent links instead of 7 (run-20) and 10 of 17 instead of 0 (run-cold-10), in about 0.1 s per link.
