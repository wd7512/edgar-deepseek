Bounded Levenberg–Marquardt now runs in chunks of 10 iterations and drops fits that have stopped between chunks. Before, the whole batch kept computing until its slowest fit finished. One equation on 791 neurons with 5 starts takes 12.8 s instead of 31.1 s (G5), 22.4 s instead of 52.1 s (SK9) and 15.6 s instead of 39.2 s (L8), with identical parameters. The protocol can also prune losing starts after a few iterations (`prune_after`, off by default). The benchmark shows that halves the time again but adds 0.3–0.5% regret, too much against the paper's 0.5% margin.
