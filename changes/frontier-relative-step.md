The frontier fitting protocol now caps each Levenberg–Marquardt step at 0.25 × (1 + |parameter|) instead of 0.25. Before, an amplitude could move only about 25 in 100 iterations, while real peak responses reach 87. On the nesting benchmark this cut child-worse-than-parent violations from 244 to 189 of 1,280, and from 6 to 2 with warm starts, at the same speed. The run checkpoint's `fit_protocol` is now `warm_multistart_v2`, so a run started under the old cap won't resume under the new one. The benchmark keeps the old cap as `absolute` and `absolute-warm`.
