Add `binned-2`, `binned-4` and `binned-8` fitters (and `-warm` variants) to `edgar fitbench`. Each fits the protocol to averages of 2, 4 or 8 angle-sorted neighbouring training trials. On 64 cells they save only 10–20% of the time, because compilation dominates. They are also far less accurate: the larger equations put spikes between the averaged points, and the best binned fitter (`binned-8-warm`) has 2.79% mean regret against 0.45% for `warm`. The frontier keeps fitting raw trials.
