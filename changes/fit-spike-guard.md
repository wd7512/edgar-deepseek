Guard fits against spikes between training angles. A start whose curve leaves its neuron's training-response range (widened by that range on each side) anywhere on a 0.05° grid now loses to any start that stays inside it. In run-guided-10 one neuron's fit put a 10⁸-high needle 0.33° from the nearest training trial; one held-out trial 0.21° from it turned an equation scoring about 28.57 into 1009.8.
