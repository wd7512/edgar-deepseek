FIGURE 7 · CAN rcvPCA RECOVER A KNOWN SPECTRUM? (paper Fig. S1)

    
Validation, and a bias in the paper's recipe

    
Ground truth: PCA of the paper model's fitted curves. We add the recording's own trial-to-trial noise and run rcvPCA. The paper's recipe (residual from the repeat mean) makes repeats' noise negatively correlated, which pushes the tail below zero and steepens α; rotating each repeat's noise to a different direction removes that.

    

      ground truth (noise-free PCA)
      rcvPCA, independent noise
      rcvPCA, paper's noise recipe
    

    

  


  

    
FIGURE 8 · ABLATIONS (paper Fig. 3e)

    
Which ingredients earn their parameters

    
Median per-cell gain in held-out R² over the double Gaussian, on all 2,110 reliable cells. Our R² is over held-out trials, so trial noise caps it; the paper split along the stimulus axis instead.
